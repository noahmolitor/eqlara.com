Data ownership · Built on Base

Own the value of your consumer data.

Eqlara is a platform where you contribute your own shopping and spending data, it is anonymized and combined into cohort-level datasets, and you earn $EQL for taking part. Every rule the system follows lives in open-source smart contracts that anyone can read.

How it works

You decide what you share. The process is the same every time.

No background tracking and no browser extension. You submit specific, consented information, and you can withdraw it.

01

Contribute

Share a demographic profile, spending categories, and receipts you choose to upload. Nothing is collected passively.

02

Anonymize

Direct identifiers — name, address, card numbers — are stripped before your contribution goes any further.

03

Aggregate

Contributions are combined into cohort-level datasets. Individual records are never the unit that leaves the platform.

04

Earn $EQL

Each accepted contribution mints $EQL to your wallet. The mint is recorded on-chain and visible to anyone.

Technology

Built in the open.

The token and the reserve contract it works with are open source and verified on-chain. Nothing about how the system behaves is hidden in a private database.

  • $EQL is a standard ERC-20 token on Base.
  • A separate reserve contract holds a USDC balance and defines how redemption works, entirely in code.
  • Both contracts are verified — the exact source behind each address is public.
  • Access control, minting rules, and every state change are enforced by the contracts, not by Eqlara's servers.
  • The current deployment is on Base Sepolia, Base's test network, while the system is still a prototype.

About $EQL

What the token is — and what it isn't.

$EQL is an ERC-20 token on Base that you earn by contributing data. Its behaviour is defined entirely by the open-source contracts linked above.

Eqlara is at prototype stage. $EQL currently exists only on Base's test network and has no monetary value. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any token, security, or financial instrument. The token's structure and any future distribution are subject to legal review before the project moves beyond testing.

Data & privacy

How the platform is designed to handle your data.

What is collected

  • Only what you actively submit in the contribution flow
  • A demographic profile you fill in yourself
  • Spending categories and receipts you choose to upload
  • Your wallet address, to send you $EQL

What the platform does not do

  • No passive tracking, no browser extension, no ad pixels
  • No sale or sharing of personal identifiers
  • Receipts have names and card numbers stripped before use
  • You can withdraw consent and request deletion at any time

A full privacy policy will be published before the platform opens to the public.

About

Why we're building this.

Consumer data is one of the largest asset classes in the economy, and the people who generate it see almost none of the value. The companies in the middle keep it because they control the pipes, not because they earned it.

Eqlara is an attempt at a different arrangement: you decide what data you contribute, it is anonymized and pooled with everyone else's, and the mechanics of how contributors are compensated are written into public code rather than a terms-of-service page. It is early, and we are building it carefully and in the open.
